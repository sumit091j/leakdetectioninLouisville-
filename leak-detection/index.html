Signs You Need Leak Detection

  • An unexplained increase in your water bill
  • The sound of running water when nothing is in use
  • Damp or discolored spots on walls, ceilings, or flooring
  • A musty smell without an obvious source
  • Low water pressure throughout the home
  • A water meter that keeps moving with everything turned off

Common Sources of Hidden Leaks

  • A slab leak in a supply line running under the foundation
  • A corroded or cracked pipe inside a wall
  • A failing fitting or joint behind a fixture
  • An underground line damaged by ground shifting or root intrusion
  • A worn toilet flapper or fill valve causing a silent, continuous leak

Our Leak Detection Process

How We Detect a Leak Without Guesswork Demolition

Rather than opening walls or flooring at random, we use listening equipment and, where applicable, camera or thermal methods to narrow down the leak's location before any access work begins.

Slab Leaks

A slab leak occurs in a supply line running beneath a home's foundation. These can be identified through pressure testing and acoustic detection, often without needing to break up the entire slab.

Leaks Behind Walls

Leaks inside wall cavities are often first noticed through moisture, discoloration, or a musty smell before any visible water appears.

What Happens After the Leak Is Found

Once located, we discuss access and repair options — this may connect to Pipe Repair or Burst Pipe Repair depending on severity.

Leak Detection FAQs

How can I tell if I have a hidden leak?+

Watch for a rising water bill with no clear cause, the sound of running water when fixtures are off, or a water meter that keeps moving with everything shut off.

Can you find a leak without cutting into my walls or slab?+

In many cases, acoustic and other non-invasive detection methods can narrow the location significantly before any access work is needed.

What is a slab leak?+

A slab leak is a leak in a water line running beneath a home's foundation, often identified through pressure testing and listening equipment.

How much water can a hidden leak waste?+

Even a small continuous leak can waste a significant amount of water over time, which is often reflected in a rising water bill.
