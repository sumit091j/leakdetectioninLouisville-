Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Leak Detection In Louisville ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or contact us to request plumbing services in Louisville, KY and the surrounding Louisville Metro area. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, phone number, email address, service address, and any details you enter into our contact or service request forms.
  • Information collected automatically — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and general location data (city/region level), gathered through standard web server logs and analytics tools.
  • Communications — records of calls, emails, or messages you send us in connection with a service request, kept for scheduling and customer service purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to service requests, quotes, and general inquiries
  • Schedule and coordinate plumbing appointments
  • Communicate with you about your request, including by phone, email, or text
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Maintain business records as required for accounting, insurance, or legal purposes

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as Google Analytics or call-tracking numbers) to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the effectiveness of our marketing. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not function as intended if you do so.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate this website, manage scheduling, or provide analytics (bound by confidentiality obligations)
  • Professional advisors, such as accountants or insurers, where necessary to run our business
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, accounting, or insurance obligations, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete your information, subject to any legal or business record-keeping requirements
  • Opt out of marketing communications by replying "stop" to texts or unsubscribing from emails

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy
