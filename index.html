After-hours issue?

When a Plumbing Problem Becomes an Emergency

Some plumbing problems can wait for a scheduled visit. Others can't. Contact us right away if you're dealing with:

Burst Pipes

Active water flow that won't stop, especially near flooring or electrical.

Major Leaks

Water pooling, ceiling stains, or a spiking water bill with no clear cause.

Overflowing Toilets

Repeated overflow can point to a deeper drain or sewer line blockage.

Sewer Backups

Multiple drains backing up at once is a main line, not a single-fixture, issue.

No Hot Water

Could be the thermostat, heating element, gas supply, or a failing tank.

Broken Fixtures

A shut-off valve that won't turn, or a fixture that's failed completely.

FAQ

Common Questions

How quickly can a plumber arrive in Louisville?+

Response time depends on current scheduling and the urgency of the issue. Call +1 (844)748-1028 and describe the problem so we can give you an accurate estimate.

What counts as a plumbing emergency?+

Active flooding, burst pipes, sewer backups affecting multiple fixtures, and complete loss of hot water are typically treated as urgent. See our Emergency Plumbing page for detail.

Why does my drain keep clogging?+

Recurring clogs are often caused by grease buildup, hair, or a partial blockage further down the line, including possible root intrusion. See Drain Cleaning.

How do I know if my sewer line is damaged?+

Multiple slow drains, gurgling, sewer odor, and soggy patches in the yard can all point to a sewer line issue. See Sewer Line Repair.

Can you detect a hidden leak without tearing out walls?+

In many cases, yes. See Leak Detection for the methods we use.

Do you work on both homes and businesses in Louisville?+

Yes, Leak Detection In Louisville handles residential and commercial plumbing throughout Louisville and Louisville Metro.
