Plumbing FAQs

Answers to the questions Louisville customers ask most.

How quickly can a plumber arrive?+

Response time depends on current scheduling and the urgency of the issue. Call +1 (502) 930-3163 for an accurate estimate.

What counts as a plumbing emergency?+

Active flooding, burst pipes, sewer backups affecting multiple fixtures, and complete loss of hot water are typically treated as urgent.

Why does my drain keep clogging?+

Recurring clogs are usually a sign of buildup further down the line, or a partial blockage rather than a one-time issue.

What causes low water pressure?+

Low pressure can come from mineral buildup in pipes, a partially closed valve, a hidden leak, or issues with the municipal supply.

How do I know if my sewer line is damaged?+

Multiple slow or backing-up drains, gurgling, sewer odor, and soggy yard patches can all point to a sewer line issue.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?+

It depends on the unit's age, extent of the problem, and cost of repair versus replacement — we'll walk through the trade-offs on site.

What causes a water heater to leak?+

Leaks can come from a failed pressure relief valve, a corroded tank, or loose fittings.

Can plumbers detect hidden leaks?+

Yes — using listening equipment and other non-invasive methods, hidden leaks can often be located without unnecessary demolition.

Do you serve areas outside Louisville?+

We serve Louisville, KY and surrounding communities including Jeffersontown, St. Matthews, Fern Creek, Okolona, New Albany, IN. Call to confirm coverage for your address.

Do you work on both residential and commercial properties?+

Yes, Leak Detection In Louisville handles residential and commercial plumbing throughout Louisville and Louisville Metro.
