Professional Drain Cleaning in Louisville, Kentucky

Slow drains and repeated clogs are usually a sign of a blockage building further down the line. Leak Detection In Louisville clears kitchen, bathroom, and main drains for homes and businesses throughout Louisville, KY.

Signs You Need Drain Cleaning

  • Water draining slower than usual in a sink, tub, or shower
  • Frequent clogs in the same drain
  • Gurgling sounds coming from pipes
  • Persistent odors near a drain
  • Standing water that won't clear
  • Multiple fixtures backing up at once

Common Causes of Drain Clogs

  • Grease and cooking oil buildup in kitchen drains
  • Hair and soap buildup in bathroom drains
  • Food particles and foreign objects
  • Tree root intrusion into underground lines
  • Mineral deposits narrowing pipe diameter over time

Drain Cleaning Methods

Depending on the blockage, we use drain snaking for localized clogs, camera inspection to confirm location and cause, or hydro jetting for grease buildup, scale, and root intrusion. See Hydro Jetting.

Kitchen Drain Cleaning

Kitchen drains are most often affected by grease and food particle buildup. Recurring kitchen clogs can usually be resolved with proper clearing and a few maintenance habits.

Bathroom Drain Cleaning

Bathroom sink, tub, and shower drains typically clog from hair and soap residue accumulating over time.

Main Drain Cleaning

A main drain issue affects multiple fixtures simultaneously and often requires professional-grade equipment to clear.

Drain Cleaning vs. Sewer Problems

If clearing a single drain doesn't resolve the issue, or multiple drains are affected, the problem may be further down in the sewer line. See Sewer Line Repair and Sewer Cleaning.

Drain Cleaning FAQs

Why does my drain keep clogging?+

Recurring clogs often mean buildup further down the line, a partial blockage, or root intrusion rather than a one-time issue.

Is drain snaking or hydro jetting better?+

Snaking works well for localized clogs; hydro jetting is better suited for grease buildup, scale, and root intrusion along longer sections of pipe.

Can tree roots really get into my pipes?+

Yes — roots are drawn to moisture and can enter through small cracks or joints in underground pipe, gradually restricting flow.

Do you use a camera to inspect drains?+

Camera inspection is used when needed to confirm the exact location and cause of a blockage before recommending a fix.
